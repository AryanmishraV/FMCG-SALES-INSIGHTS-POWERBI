# FMCG Sales Insights – Power BI

## Business Problem
FMCG organizations generate large volumes of sales data but often lack clear visibility
into product performance, regional demand, and revenue trends. This project focuses on
transforming raw sales data into actionable business insights for decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies
- SQL – data extraction, joins, aggregations
- Excel – data cleaning and validation
- Power BI – interactive dashboards and KPI tracking

## Key KPIs Analyzed
- Total Revenue and Monthly Sales Trend
- Top and Bottom Performing Products
- Regional Sales Performance
- Category-wise Sales Contribution
- Sales Growth Patterns

## Dashboard Overview
The Power BI dashboard enables:
- Drill-down analysis by region and product
- Identification of high and low performing SKUs
- Trend analysis to support sales planning
- Data-driven operational and sales decisions

## Business Insights
- Identified products contributing the majority of revenue
- Highlighted underperforming regions requiring action
- Provided visibility into category-level performance
- Enabled management to prioritize high-impact areas

## Outcome
This project demonstrates how SQL, Excel, and Power BI can be used together to
support real-world FMCG business decisions through data-driven insights.
